How to Contact Big Fish Games Customer Service
-----------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games provides customer support through its online Help Center. Customers can use the support center to find answers to common questions or submit a request to the customer service team.


What Big Fish Games Customer Service Can Help With
---------------------------------------------------

Customer support can help with various issues involving Big Fish Games products and services, including:

* Account and sign-in problems
* Game-related technical issues
* Purchases and billing questions
* Missing or incorrect items
* Subscription-related concerns
* Refund questions
* Problems accessing purchased games
* Account deletion requests
* Other questions about Big Fish Games products and services

When contacting support, provide relevant information such as your account email address, game name, device details, purchase information, and a description of the problem.

Refund Requests
---------------

Refund procedures may depend on where the purchase was made.

For purchases made directly through Big Fish Games, contact Big Fish Customer Support regarding the applicable refund process.

For purchases made through third-party platforms such as the Apple App Store or Google Play, refund requests generally need to be handled through the applicable platform.

Account Support
---------------

If you are having trouble with your Big Fish Games account, use the official support center to find account-related assistance.

For account deletion requests, customers can submit an online Customer Support request.

Tips for Contacting Customer Service
------------------------------------

When submitting a support request, provide clear and accurate information about your issue.

Useful details may include:

* Your Big Fish Games account email address
* The name of the affected game
* Your device and operating system
* The date and amount of a relevant purchase
* Order or transaction information
* Screenshots or error messages
* A description of what happened
* Steps you have already tried

Do not provide sensitive information such as your password or complete payment-card number.
